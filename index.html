Sera

Sera is a private tool that SYLO, a virtual assistant business, uses to make sure nothing slips through the cracks for its clients.

What it does

Each morning, Sera reads the Gmail inboxes and Google Calendars that SYLO's clients have given it access to. It then prepares one briefing email for SYLO's virtual assistant, covering the day's meetings, messages that need a reply, follow-ups and deadlines.

Who it's for

Sera is not a public product. It is used only by SYLO, and only with accounts belonging to SYLO and its clients, each of whom chose to connect.

Your data

Client accounts are connected read-only. Sera never sends, deletes or changes anything in a client's mailbox or calendar. Read the privacy policy for the details.
